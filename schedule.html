FAQ

Who can attend?

Anyone!

How do I attend?

  1. sign up with the form below
  2. show up to room 1037 after school on the 19th
  3. play some games and have fun!

How long will the event last?

Up to 3 hours.

Is this free?

Yes, this event is completely free to attend! Feel free to bring your friends and family, and some snacks.

Do I need to bring my own controller?

We should have enough controllers for everyone, but you are welcome to use your own!

What else is there at the event?

There are other things to do, besides playing Smash:

  • computers (for gaming)
  • retro arcade machines
  • PlayStation and XBox (with games)

What do I bring?

Bring:

  1. an ID of any kind
  2. snacks
  3. friends (to watch)
  4. your own controller (optional)

Don't bring:

  • your own console
  • any recording device

I've never played Smash before!

This event is entirely for fun! Even if you don't make it anywhere in the bracket, you can still have a great time!

Tournament Format

The tournament will likely be a single-elimination bracket, with as many participants as possible! Later rounds might be best of 3 or 5, maybe double elimination.
